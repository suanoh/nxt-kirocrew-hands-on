# Department AI Hands-on Meetup

## Summary

The department is hosting a hands-on meetup where students use AI together directly, with a capacity of 12 people and open even to students who are completely new to AI practice; the English and Japanese notices are provided only to help you understand the meetup information, and regardless of the translation you read, the meetup itself is conducted entirely in Korean, with both the guidance and the hands-on practice carried out in Korean.
